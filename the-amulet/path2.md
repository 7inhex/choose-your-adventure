# Path 2, Day 1

As you walk around the house, the hills greet you with a beautiful sunset. It's captivating. Walking towards the hills, you feel comforted, surrounded by a warmth, embraced by the air. Wait... not the air. The ground.

You lay down and... connect. As you press against the dirt, it feels like something is projecting through your body from the ground - like something reaching through you.
